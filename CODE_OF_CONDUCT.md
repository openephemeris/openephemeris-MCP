# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- Being welcoming and respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Behavior that isn't acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Astrology, Human Design, and divinatory systems attract strong opinions about validity. Skepticism is welcome and interesting; contempt for other participants is not. Argue with the claim, not the person.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair corrective action in response to any unacceptable behavior — including editing or removing comments, commits, issues, and other contributions, and temporarily or permanently banning contributors.

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to **support@openephemeris.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of anyone reporting an incident.
